# Relearning

Today is my birthday! Yay! 🎉, I have completed one more round around the sun. It is indeed a joyous day for me because as of today, I
can proudly say that I have been able to scratch a few of the things that were in my bucket list for a very long time. The most
important one being the PC that I wanted to build for myself. I got the PC built and am able to dual boot both Linux(my main programming
driver) as well as Windows (for occasional gaming). Apart from that, I have also been able to accrue some of the hardware at the prices
which they were meant to be sold at. Currently the prices of these hardware have appreciated by ~40%.

Having said that, now that I have my machines ready, it is time for me to get back to something that I love doing - `Programming`. This
is the one thing that has always been with me, through thick and thin I have found solace in programming. Even in the advent of AI, I
still find myself drawn to programming, reading books and writing(code/posts/anything for that matter).

Hence on this day, I thought of restarting my programming journey by doing the following:
 - Reading up on C (Starting from K&R - C)
 - Reading up on C++ (Starting from C++ Primer)
 - Reading up on Python (Starting from Beginning Python - From Novice to Professional to Fluent Python)
 - Restarting to learn the following languages:
  - Lua
  - Go-Lang


## Why going back to the basics?

Why am I going back to reading the basics of the languages I already know and have been using? The reason is simple. With the advent of
AI, there are a lot of peculiar codes that I go through sometimes, this causes my knowledge to falter. It is something along the lines of
"If a lie is told multiple times, it becomes the truth". I have been seeing a lot of code that is not written in the best practices
and it is causing my knowledge to falter - I think...

In order to make sure that my basics are still good enough, I will be re-embarking on the journey of re-reading through the basics of the
languages that I absolutely love to work with - `C` & `C++`. Apart from that, I have always found `Python` to be the most easy to use
language for prototyping my ideas. In certain situations, I have written scripts which have allowed me to perform certain operations. It
would have been better to write the same functionality using `C` or `C++`, however, I have been lazy and wrote the same using `Python`.

Hence, I am going to re-read the basics and this time onward try to keep working on the projects that I have planned.


## Why restart learning Lua and Go?

Well, with respect to Go and Lua, there is this thing where I started with going through the basics and had to leave the path on account of
certain other priority tasks. I have never been able to complete either Go-Lang or Lua. I have been always been fascinated by the power of
Lua and of late Go-lang. I have been of late writing a lot of small utility tools using Go-lang in office and have fallen in love with
the language. The love might have been also affected by the creators/designers of the language - Rob Pike, Ken Thompson and Robert Griesemer.


## So, what are the plans?

The plans are as follows:
 - I will definitely go through the basics of the languages that I know.
 - I will definitely learn Lua and Go-lang by the time I complete one more revolution around the sun.

 My tooling is now complete, I am using NeoVim as my main editor, tmux as my terminal emulator, zsh as my shell and Ghostty as the terminal
emulator.

With all that said, wish me luck...
